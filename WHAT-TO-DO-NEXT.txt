Now that Starter Pack is installed, do this next.

1. Make one folder for your first project.
Example names:
- My First Site
- My School App
- My Business Website
- My Family Organizer

2. Open Codex or Claude Code.

3. Open that one folder as your working folder.
This matters: the agent works inside the folder you opened.

4. Then say one of these:
- Plan and build me a simple website for my business. Start with a plan, then build it step by step.
- Plan and build me a simple app for tracking tasks. Start with a plan, then build it step by step.
- Plan and make me a short presentation for parents night at school. Start with a clear outline, then build the slides.
- Help me convert this scanned Hebrew PDF into Markdown without translating or summarizing it. Keep page structure and uncertainties.
- Make me a simple landing page. Explain what you are doing in plain language.
- Make me a small app and show me how to run, preview, and publish it.
- Build me a simple website. Start with a plan, use frontend-design for the first version, then do a design-elevation pass before finalizing it.
- Build me a simple website, then review the UI and improve anything confusing, ugly, or hard to use.
- Build me a simple site, check it in a real browser, and help me put it online with a preview link.

5. Do not worry about code.
You can ask in normal language.

Good first rule:
Ask the agent to plan first, then build.

Example:
Plan a simple presentation for my tutoring business. First outline the story, then make the slides in a clean simple style I can really use.
